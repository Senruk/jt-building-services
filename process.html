Our Process

How we deliver commercial projects

Every project follows the same proven structure. It keeps us accountable and gives you complete visibility from day one.

01

Enquiry

You tell us about your project: scope, location, timeline, budget. Call, email or use the contact form. We'll acknowledge within 24 hours.

02

Survey

We visit site to take measurements, assess access and services, and identify any constraints. This is free and carries no obligation.

03

Quote

You receive a fixed-price written quotation with a full scope of works, programme duration, payment schedule and our standard terms. No hidden costs.

04

Programme

Once instructed, we issue a detailed project programme showing every phase, milestone and completion date. You'll know what happens and when.

05

Delivery

Our in-house team delivers the work. One project manager, direct trades, no subcontractors. Regular progress updates and site meetings throughout.

06

Handover

Final inspection, snagging if needed, certificate pack and practical completion. Your project is signed off and we remain available for any follow-up.

Why It Works

Structure creates certainty.

Commercial clients value predictability. Our six-step process means you're never guessing what comes next. Every phase has clear deliverables, and you always know who's responsible.

Because we keep every trade in-house, there are no handoffs between subcontractors, no coordination gaps, and no one to blame but ourselves if something slips. That's the accountability commercial projects require.
