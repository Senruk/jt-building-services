Commercial capabilities

Commercial Fit-Outs & Refurbishment

Full-scope commercial fit-out and refurbishment for offices, retail and industrial premises. We manage everything from initial survey through design, construction and handover, delivered on programme, to specification.

  • Office fit-out and refurbishment
  • Retail and hospitality fit-out
  • Industrial unit refurbishment
  • Category A and B fit-out
  • Live-site delivery (zero disruption)
  • Full project management

Office, Retail & Industrial Units

Specialist fit-out and refurbishment for commercial premises across all sectors. We deliver clean, compliant spaces on live sites, with minimal disruption to your operations.

  • Office spaces and headquarters
  • Retail and hospitality premises
  • Warehouse and industrial units
  • Commercial kitchen installations
  • Phased delivery for live sites
  • Complete M&E integration

Commercial Groundworks & New Builds

Ground-up commercial construction from foundations to handover. In-house capability across earthworks, foundations, drainage, slab works and structural shell. Single-point accountability on every contract.

  • Site clearance and earthworks
  • Foundations and substructures
  • Drainage and utilities
  • Reinforced concrete slabs
  • Structural steel and frame
  • Turnkey commercial delivery

Commercial M&E Services

Mechanical and electrical design, installation and certification for commercial premises. All work complies with current regulations and is fully certified upon completion.

  • Commercial electrical installations
  • HVAC and ventilation systems
  • Lighting design and controls
  • Fire alarm and safety systems
  • Data and comms cabling
  • Testing and certification

Project Management & Delivery

End-to-end project management for commercial construction projects. Single point of contact, in-house trades across every discipline, full programme and budget accountability.

  • Full project management
  • Programme and budget control
  • In-house multi-trade delivery
  • Site safety and compliance
  • Quality assurance
  • Client reporting and handover

Structural Works & Shell Construction

Structural works including steel frame erection, reinforced concrete, masonry and structural carpentry. Delivered to engineering specifications, on programme.

  • Steel frame erection
  • Reinforced concrete works
  • Structural masonry
  • Roof structures and cladding
  • Staircase and mezzanine installs
  • Structural alterations

External Works & Infrastructure

Site infrastructure, external works and hard landscaping for commercial premises. Drainage, utility connections, access roads, parking and finished hardstanding.

  • Drainage and attenuation
  • Utility connections
  • Access roads and parking
  • Hardstanding and slab works
  • Fencing and security
  • Landscaping and finished levels

Design, Planning & Compliance

Planning applications, architectural drawings, structural calculations and building regulations approval for commercial projects. We handle the paperwork so your project stays on track.

  • Planning applications
  • Architectural drawings
  • Structural engineering
  • Building regulations
  • CDM compliance
  • Health and safety documentation

Insurance Works & Reinstatement

Repair and reinstatement of commercial premises following insured damage. We work directly with insurers, loss adjusters and loss assessors to restore your building to its pre-loss condition, quickly and to standard.

  • Fire damage repair and reinstatement
  • Flood and escape of water damage
  • Storm damage repairs
  • Subsidence and structural repairs
  • Emergency protection and temporary works
  • Working with insurers and loss adjusters
