Building capabilities

From planning permission to final handover. We manage the entire process.

Extensions

Rear, side, wraparound and two-storey extensions. We handle everything from initial design through planning, structural calculations and construction. Open-plan kitchen diners, extra bedrooms, home offices, you name it.

  • Rear and side extensions
  • Wraparound and two-storey
  • Kitchen diner extensions
  • Planning and building regs
  • Structural calculations
  • Full project management

Renovations

Full property renovations, kitchen and bathroom refits, loft conversions and basement conversions. We transform tired spaces into modern, functional homes. Every trade in-house means no delays between stages.

  • Full house renovations
  • Kitchen and bathroom refits
  • Loft conversions
  • Basement conversions
  • Open-plan layouts
  • Period property restoration

New Builds

From single-storey annexes to bespoke family homes and residential developments. We handle groundworks, foundations, shell construction, first and second fix. Your project, managed end to end.

  • Bespoke family homes
  • Annexes and granny flats
  • Residential developments
  • Design and build
  • Groundworks and foundations
  • Turnkey delivery

Property Development

  • Site assessment

Plumbing

Full plumbing services including new bathrooms, boiler installations, central heating, pipework, leak detection and repairs. All work by qualified, registered plumbers to current building regulations.

  • Bathroom installations

Electrical

Roofing & Exteriors
