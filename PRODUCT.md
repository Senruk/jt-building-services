# Product

## Register

brand

## Users
Commercial decision-makers in South Wales (Barry, Vale of Glamorgan, Cardiff and surrounding areas) — business owners, facilities managers, landlords, and developers who need construction work delivered: fit-outs, groundworks, M&E, structural works, refurbishment, or insurance repairs. They are evaluating a contractor as much as a website: they want proof of capability, evidence of delivered work, and a clear sense they can trust the in-house team to manage the job from start to finish.

## Product Purpose

JT.BS South Wales Ltd is an established (2013) professional building contractor. The website exists to convert visitors into project enquiries and calls. Success looks like: a visitor sees the portfolio and the way the company works, trusts the quality and programme certainty, and submits an enquiry or phones 07514 898494. The site must feel like an established contractor, not a start-up offering.

## Brand Personality

Solid, confident, understated. The voice of a contractor who has done this for years and doesn't need to shout. Competence over flash; work speaks ahead of words.

## Anti-references

- Generic corporate-template construction sites (stock-photo hero handshakes, clipart hard hats, generic "quality, safety, value" three-word tridents).
- Cream/terracotta or broadsheet "renovation studio" aesthetics — this is commercial construction, not interiors blogging.
- Oversized hero-metric SaaS clichés and gradient-text marketing tricks that undermine the understated-pro contractor feel.

## Design Principles

- Work over words: photography and delivered projects carry the proof; copy stays lean and specific.
- Quiet confidence: disciplined black palette, restrained use of accent, minimal decoration — the brand says "we've done this before".
- Enquiry as the load-bearing goal: every section nudges toward a clear next step (form or phone), never dead-ends.
- In-house honesty: "delivered by our in-house team" appears in language and structure — the team is the product.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA as a working target: ≥4.5:1 body contrast, ≥3:1 large text, visible focus states.
- prefers-reduced-motion support for all animation (scroll reveals, carousel, stat counters).
- Semantic landmarks, skip links, descriptive alt text on all imagery.
