Who we are

A building company built on reputation, not advertising. Nearly a decade of quality construction across the Vale of Glamorgan.

Our Story

From small beginnings to established contractors

J.T Building Services started in 2016 with a simple philosophy: do the job right, charge a fair price, and let the work speak for itself. We began with kitchen and bathroom refits in Barry and grew through word-of-mouth into a full-scope building company.

Today we employ a direct team of 12 skilled tradespeople covering groundworks, brickwork, roofing, joinery, plumbing and electrical. Every project, from a single-storey extension to a bespoke new build, is managed in-house with the same attention to detail as our first job.

We don't subcontract. We don't cut corners. We don't disappear when the job's done.
