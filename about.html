Who we are

A commercial construction company built on delivery certainty, not advertising. In-house trades, single-point accountability, established since 2013.

Our Story

From small beginnings to established commercial contractors

JT.BS South Wales Ltd started in 2013 with a simple philosophy: deliver quality work, charge a fair price, and let the results speak for themselves. We began with smaller projects in Barry and grew through repeat business and referrals into a full-scope commercial construction company.

Today we employ a direct team of 12 skilled tradespeople covering groundworks, structure, M&E and fit-out: every discipline in-house. Every project, from a retail fit-out to a ground-up commercial new build, is managed end to end with a single point of accountability.

We don't subcontract. We don't cut corners. We don't disappear when the job's done.
